Short biography

Norman Borlaug was an American agronomist who led initiatives worldwide that contributed to the extensive increases in agricultural production. Because of his work, millions of people were saved from hunger.

Important years

  • 1914 - Born in Iowa, USA
  • 1940s - Began research in plant pathology
  • 1960s - Led the Green Revolution
  • 1970 - Won Nobel Peace Prize
  • 2009 - Passed away
